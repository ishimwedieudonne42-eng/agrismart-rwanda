Purpose of this page

This page documents the published data source behind AgriSmart Rwanda and explains how selected tables are used by the prototype. The project uses prepared CSV files derived from NISR tables; users should consult the original publication for full definitions, survey notes, and context.

Primary source: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR), Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) 2025, Season B.

3. Data handling and interpretation rules

  • Use the survey year and season specified by the dataset; the current district explorer focuses on 2025 Season B.
  • Missing values are not automatically treated as zero. A blank or unavailable value should remain distinct from a reported zero.
  • The district yield explorer ranks positive reported yield values; zero and missing values are not included in positive-yield rankings until verified against the original table.
  • Crop categories may overlap. Do not add crop totals together unless the source confirms that the categories are mutually exclusive.
  • District comparisons are descriptive. They do not show that agricultural practices caused yield or erosion differences.
  • Check units carefully: district crop yield is displayed in kilograms per hectare (kg/ha); national crop yield in the national dashboard is displayed in metric tonnes per hectare (t/ha).

4. Suggested citation

National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR). Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025, Season B. Consult the official catalogue for the publication and supporting documentation.

https://microdatabk.statistics.gov.rw/catalog/124

When presenting results, state which table, year, season, indicator, and unit you used. Do not describe an association or difference as a cause unless supported by appropriate evidence.
