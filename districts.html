How to interpret this chart

Source: NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025, Season B, Table 11. Yield is displayed in kilograms per hectare (kg/ha).

Entries with zero or missing yield are excluded from the chart until their meaning is verified against the original table. A higher reported yield does not by itself explain why a district performs differently.
