Important interpretation notes

Data quality is not the same as data accuracy. This page measures whether values are present and whether district names match across prepared files. It cannot independently verify the original survey responses or explain why values are missing.
  • Blank or unavailable cells remain missing; they are not converted to zero.
  • Different tables may cover different topics, populations, or denominators.
  • Check definitions and methodology in the official source before making decisions.
