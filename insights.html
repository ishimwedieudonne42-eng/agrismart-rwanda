How to interpret these indicators

These charts show district-level descriptive statistics. They do not prove that any one practice caused or prevented a particular erosion outcome. The practices and erosion measures may use different denominators and survey definitions.

Data note: Figures are from the NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025, Season B, Tables 29 and 33. Percentages are displayed as reported. Missing cells remain unavailable; zero is retained only where the source reports zero.
