4. How to use these suggestions

  1. Use the comparison to decide which topic may deserve closer attention in the selected district.
  2. Ask local farmers and extension officers about soil conditions, water access, crop varieties, input availability, and other relevant factors.
  3. Check the original NISR tables and additional local evidence before deciding on an intervention.
  4. Track outcomes over time before concluding that a practice caused a change in yield or erosion.

Important: A district's indicator being above or below the median does not establish why it is different, and it does not prove that one practice caused a yield or erosion outcome. Suitability, cost, water availability, land conditions, and farmer preferences must be assessed locally.
