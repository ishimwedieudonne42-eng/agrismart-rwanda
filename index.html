Agricultural data intelligence

Turn Rwanda's agricultural statistics into clearer insights.

AgriSmart Rwanda is a student-built data exploration platform using published National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) agricultural survey tables. Explore crop production, compare reported yields across districts, and review agricultural practice and erosion indicators.

Selected findings: Season B 2024–2025

These comparisons use NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025, Season B, Table 6. Percentages show change in reported production from 2024 to 2025, calculated from the processed source table.

🌱 +8.84%

Cassava production increased

Reported production rose from about 783,294 tonnes in 2024 to 852,541 tonnes in 2025.

🍌 +2.07%

Banana production increased

Reported production rose from about 1,142,552 tonnes in 2024 to 1,166,247 tonnes in 2025.

🍠 −2.14%

Sweet potato production decreased

Reported production fell from about 666,814 tonnes in 2024 to 652,515 tonnes in 2025.

🌽 −1.17%

Maize production decreased

Reported production fell from about 119,101 tonnes in 2024 to 117,711 tonnes in 2025.

How to read these findings: These are descriptive year-to-year changes in reported production, not explanations of why changes occurred. Consider harvested area and yield alongside production. Source: NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025 catalogue.
