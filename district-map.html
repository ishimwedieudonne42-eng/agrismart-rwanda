How to use this map

  1. Choose an indicator from the dropdown.
  2. For crop yield, choose a crop.
  3. Click a district marker to view the district name, selected value and other available indicators.
  4. Use “Show all districts” to return to the whole-country view.

Interpretation note: This map uses district reference-point coordinates, not district boundary polygons. Marker locations are approximate district locations and should not be interpreted as the exact geographic extent of a district.

District coordinate source: Rwanda Administrative Divisions dataset (CC BY 4.0). Agricultural data source: NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025 catalogue. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
